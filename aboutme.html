All About me

Hi, my name is Monireh and I am the provider of licensed childcare program, Future Stars. I’m married and I have a son and a little girl. Throughout my life I have dedicated a lot of my time to taking care of children and I must say I enjoy every minute of it. I have been working in the field of early childhood education and development since 2017. I decided to open a home daycare because I have a great passion for taking care of children. I believe children are the foundation of society and are the future. I understand that in the early years the most important things that children need are love and security. I find a great deal of happiness fulfilling their needs. I’m so proud and excited to work with children.
